# Digital PR and Press Outreach

## Overview

Digital PR is the highest-leverage white-hat SEO strategy to earn Domain Authority (DA 50+) editorial backlinks from mainstream news outlets and tech blogs (ProPakistani, TechJuice, Dawn Aurora, Profit Pakistan, Geo, Tribune).

---

## Media Pitch Template

```text
Subject: DATA: How much Pakistani YouTube & TVC Video Editors Earn in 2026 (New Industry Report)

Hi [Editor/Reporter Name],

With Pakistan ranking among the world's largest digital freelance workforces, we just released the first open-access benchmark report on the post-production economy:

Key Takeaways:
- Average rate for 10-20 min YouTube video: PKR 35,000.
- 62% of creators now use After Effects as a secondary motion tool.
- Direct WhatsApp hiring grew 140% YoY as creators avoid 20% platform escrow cuts.

Full report and charts available with open citation: https://pakiedit.com/report-2026

Happy to provide regional breakdowns or commentary from top Pakistani editors.
```
