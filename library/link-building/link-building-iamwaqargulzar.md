# Link Building

Earn relevant editorial links and mentions without buying ranking credit or manufacturing endorsements. Read `references/skill-contract.md` for evidence, permissions, and handoffs; use `references/earned-link-plan.md` for tactics and verification.

## Diagnose before outreach

Identify the target pages, real audience/query intent, existing assets, relevant competitors, backlink data access, prior placements/manual actions, and available effort. Confirm crawl/indexation/content/internal-linking issues before treating links as the limiting factor.

Compare referring pages/domains at the target-page level, with dates and data coverage. Counts or proprietary domain ratings alone cannot prove an authority bottleneck or guarantee that additional links will improve rank. Use `offsite-signal-analyzer` for detailed profile diagnosis and `seo-audit` for page issues.

## Plan and qualify

Start with useful existing assets and appropriate reclamation or partner opportunities. Research the actual page, audience, editorial quality, destination fit, access limits, and a legitimate contact source. Preserve observed link attributes and placement evidence; do not invent audience traffic or editorial relationships.

For AI visibility, sample the buyer's real prompts and cited pages across engines/markets/time, not a universal list of high-authority domains. A relevant mention is a research lead, not proof that inclusion will cause an assistant to recommend the product.

Draft specific, truthful outreach with a bounded follow-up plan. Approve the recipient list, exact copy, channel, and caps before sending; the request for a link-building plan does not authorize messages, purchases, account creation, or profile changes.

## Policy and risk

Check current search spam and outbound-link guidance. Do not recommend payment, products, or excessive exchanges for links intended to manipulate ranking credit. Commercial placements need appropriate qualification/disclosure; a qualifying attribute is not a guarantee that the whole arrangement is compliant.

Avoid automated link networks, fabricated authority metrics, impersonation, and fake reviews. Assess risks at the actual site/page/transaction rather than treating a vendor label as proof of safety or fraud. Do not create a disavow file or alter it merely because a tool labels links toxic; require appropriate evidence and separate authorization.

## Deliverable

Return a page-level diagnosis with sources/unknowns, asset/tactic priorities, qualified prospects, outreach drafts, observed or proposed link qualification, costs/permissions, owner, and measurement window. Track outreach, actual placements, observed destination/rel/redirects, referral outcomes, and repeated search/AI observations separately. Never guarantee rankings, indexing, citation inclusion, or a fixed safe link velocity.

Use DONE_WITH_CONCERNS for incomplete datasets and NEEDS_INPUT for a material spending or outreach choice. Hand journalist work to `public-relations`, directories to `directory-submissions`, review programs to `referrals`, and outreach execution mechanics to `cold-email`.
