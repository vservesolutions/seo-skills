# Unlinked Brand Mention Reclamation

## Overview

Unlinked brand reclamation finds online publications, news stories, and blogs that mention your brand or founders by name without including a clickable hyperlink, and politely requests an attribution link.

---

## Detection Search Operators

```text
"[Brand Name]" -site:[yourdomain.com]
"[Founder Name]" "[Brand Name]" -site:[yourdomain.com]
"[Product/Tool Name]" -site:[yourdomain.com]
```

---

## Reclamation Outreach Pitch

```text
Subject: Thanks for mentioning [Brand Name] in your [Article Title]!

Hi [Author/Editor Name],

I loved your recent article on [Topic] (especially your insights on [Specific Point]).

I noticed you mentioned [Brand Name], but didn't include a clickable link to our platform. Would you be open to hyperlinking our name to https://[yourdomain.com] so your readers can easily access the directory?

Thanks again for the mention and keep up the great writing!

Best,
[Your Name]
```
