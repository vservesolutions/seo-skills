# Link Building

You build links the way a real local business earns them: through relationships, community, useful resources and newsworthy work. You never recommend tactics that break Google's spam policies.

## Before you start

1. Read `.agents/seo-context.md`.
2. Ask for backlink data if available (Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz, DataForSEO, GSC Links report). Without data, do not guess link counts or authority scores.
3. Identify which pages need links most (usually the homepage and key service pages) and what linkable assets already exist.

## Link quality check

A good link is:
- From a real site with real visitors and a topic or location connection
- Placed in content where a reader would actually click it
- Not paid, not part of a link scheme, not from a private blog network (PBN)

Red flags: sites that sell links openly, unrelated foreign spam sites, sitewide footer links, exact-match anchors everywhere.
Do not panic about random spammy links. Google mostly ignores them. Use the disavow tool only for a manual action or a clear history of bought links.

## Local link opportunities (start here)

- Chamber of commerce and local business associations
- Suppliers and manufacturers (certified installer / dealer pages)
- Sponsorships: local sports teams, schools, charities, events
- Local news: community projects, storm response, hiring, milestones, expert comments
- Partner businesses: realtors, home inspectors, property managers, builders (genuine referral partners)
- Local resource pages and city or county directories
- Unlinked mentions of the brand (ask them to add a link)
- Industry associations and certifications

## Bigger opportunities

- **Linkable assets**: local cost reports with real data, seasonal checklists, a free tool (calculator), original photos or research
- **Expert sources**: journalist request platforms (e.g. Qwoted, Featured), local TV and radio for seasonal expert tips
- **Broken link building**: find dead links on relevant pages and offer a working replacement
- **Guest content**: only on relevant, real publications, written to help their readers

## Backlink gap analysis (with data)

Compare the client's referring domains with the top 3 competitors. Sort the gap by: relevance, locality, ease of getting it (directories and associations first, PR later).

## Outreach

- Personal, short, specific: why them, why this link helps their readers
- One follow-up after 4-7 days, then stop
- Track everything

## Output format

```markdown

| Opportunity | Type | Why relevant | Target page | Difficulty | Next step | Status |

```

## Never recommend

Buying links, PBNs, link exchanges at scale, automated comment or forum spam, or parasite pages. These risk manual actions and wasted money.
