# Local Citations and Google Business Profile Management

## Overview

Local SEO enables platforms to capture geo-targeted searches (*"video editors near me"*, *"video production company in Lahore"*) by dominating the Google Local 3-Pack Maps carousel and localized organic rankings.

---

## NAP Standardization Formula

- **Name**: `[Brand Name] - [City / Region] [Category] Hub`
- **Address**: Full physical address or service area with postal code precision.
- **Phone**: Consistent phone/WhatsApp format.
- **URL**: Dedicated localized corridor URL (e.g. `https://domain.com/?city=Lahore`).
