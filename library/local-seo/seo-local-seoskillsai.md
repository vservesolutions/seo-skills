# Local SEO & Google Business Profile Standard

Audits and optimizes geographic relevance, Google Map Pack ranking factors, and multi-location directories.

---

## 📍 The 5 Local SEO Audit Points

1. **NAP (Name, Address, Phone) Consistency:** Zero variance across GBP, website footer, contact page, and directory listings.
2. **LocalBusiness Schema Validation:** Strict JSON-LD embedding `address`, `geo` coordinates, `openingHoursSpecification`, and `areaServed`.
3. **Multi-Location Hierarchy:** Dedicated city/neighborhood landing pages structured with unique localized content (not thin programmatic duplicates).
4. **Review Velocity & Sentiment:** Monitoring customer review cadence and sentiment keywords.
5. **Citation Tier Indexing:** Verifying presence in Tier 1 (Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp) and Tier 2 local citation aggregators.
