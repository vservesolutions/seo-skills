# Content Brief Keyword Map

## Decisions

- Cluster actual queries by intent and map each cluster to one content purpose and conversion stage.
- Distinguish demand evidence from inferred topics; avoid creating competing pages for the same intent.

## Acceptance

Deliver primary intent, source queries, outline, evidence requirements, and internal-link targets.

## Constraints

- Do not keyword-stuff or create low-value generated content.
