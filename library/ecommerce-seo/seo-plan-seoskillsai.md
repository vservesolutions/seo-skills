# Strategic SEO Roadmap & Growth Planning

Generates structured 30/60/90-day execution roadmaps customized for specific business models.

---

## 🎯 Supported Industry Playbooks

1. **SaaS / B2B Software:** Focuses on high-intent product comparison pages ("X vs Y", "Alternatives"), technical documentation `llms.txt`, and developer API indexing.
2. **E-Commerce / D2C:** Focuses on product category schema, faceted navigation indexing control, out-of-stock URL management, and Google Merchant Center feeds.
3. **Local Service Business:** Focuses on GBP map pack radius, city landing pages, and local citation tiers.
4. **Digital Publisher / Media:** Focuses on Google Discover image optimization, Media RSS feeds, E-E-A-T author bylines, and instant IndexNow pings.
5. **SEO Agency:** Focuses on automated client deliverables, prioritized audit scores, and drift regression tracking.
