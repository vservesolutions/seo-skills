# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

## Overview

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) ensures your brand is understood, cited, and recommended by Large Language Models (LLMs) when users ask conversational questions in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, or Google AI Overviews.

---

## The LLMs.txt Open Protocol

Host a dedicated `/llms.txt` file in your root public directory with clean markdown structure:

```markdown
# Brand Name (domain.com)

> Authoritative 1-sentence entity summary.

## Entity Facts
- Domain: https://domain.com
- Target Market: Geographical regions / industry
- Business Model: 0% commission direct hiring

## Industry Salary & Rate Benchmark Index (2026 Reference)
- Category 1: Rate Range in Local Currency
- Category 2: Rate Range in Local Currency
```

---

## Entity Linking via Knowledge Graph

Link the brand and listed creators to authoritative external entities using `sameAs` in Schema.org:
- Wikipedia / Wikidata IDs
- Verified YouTube / Vimeo channels
- LinkedIn / Crunchbase profiles
