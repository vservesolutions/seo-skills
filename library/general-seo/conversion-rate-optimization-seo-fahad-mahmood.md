# Conversion Rate Optimization for SEO Landing Pages

## Overview

Rankings without conversions produce zero business value. This skill ensures every organic search visitor is converted into an active client inquiry through optimized CTAs and zero-friction communication channels.

---

## Core CRO Tactics for Organic Traffic

1. **Sticky Floating WhatsApp CTAs**: Keep hiring buttons within thumb-reach on mobile screens.
2. **Interactive Rate Calculators**: Allow clients to calculate realistic project costs before reaching out.
3. **Verified Creator Badges**: Prominently display verified showreel proof and review ratings.
4. **PostHog Telemetry**: Track `whatsapp_hire_clicked` and inquiry conversion drop-offs.
