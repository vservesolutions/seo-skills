# Programmatic SEO Pages

## Decisions

- Define a real unique data/intent source for each page template before generating URLs.
- Control duplication, empty pages, canonicalization, crawl budget, and stale source data.

## Acceptance

Sample rendered pages and verify useful differentiated content plus indexability.

## Constraints

- Do not publish thin pages without unique user value.
