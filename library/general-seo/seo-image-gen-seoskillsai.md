# Contextual AI Image Generation for SEO

Guidelines for generating informational and editorial visual assets that rank in Google Images and Discover.

---

## 🎨 Asset Generation Directives

- **Informational Infographics & Architectural Diagrams:** Prioritize clear visual hierarchy, legible text callouts, and clean background contrast over abstract artistic styles.
- **Aspect Ratios:** Default to `16:9` for featured blog headers and `1:1` or `4:3` for in-content diagrams.
- **Provenance & Watermarking:** Maintain clean metadata and avoid misleading or deceptive photorealistic deepfakes.
