# Technical SEO Audit

## Decisions

- Reconcile crawl/render evidence with status, robots, canonical, sitemap, redirect, and structured-data behavior.
- Separate template-wide defects from isolated URLs and prioritize user/indexing impact.

## Acceptance

Provide affected URLs, checks, evidence dates, and explicit data-access gaps.

## Constraints

- Do not recommend manipulative or search-policy-violating tactics.
