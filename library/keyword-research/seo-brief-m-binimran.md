# seo-brief

A brief that lets a writer produce a page that actually ranks and converts.

## Process
1. **Target keyword + intent:** the primary term and what the searcher actually wants (informational /
   commercial / transactional). Verify real search terms - don't assume volume.
2. **SERP analysis:** what's ranking, the angle/format that wins, and the gap you can fill (do better/different).
3. **Outline:** H1 + H2/H3 structure covering the intent and related subtopics/questions (People Also Ask).
4. **On-page:** title tag, meta description, URL slug, primary + secondary keywords (used naturally), internal
   links, and a schema type if relevant.
5. **Conversion:** the CTA/next step for readers who are ready.

## Output
- A brief: keyword + intent, SERP insight, outline (H1-H3), on-page elements, internal links, word-count
  guidance, and the CTA.

## Guardrails
- Write for the searcher first, the engine second - no keyword stuffing or thin AI content.
- Verify keyword/volume claims with a real tool; mark assumptions `[verify]` (`truth-telling`).
