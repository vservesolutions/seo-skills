# On-Page Content and Semantic SEO

## Overview

On-page SEO structures content so that search algorithms understand entity relationships, keyword hierarchy, and user intent while compelling searchers to click through.

---

## Semantic HTML5 Hierarchy

- **`<h1>`**: Exactly one per page. Must contain the primary target entity and location/specialty modifier.
- **`<h2>`**: Major content sections (e.g. *Verified Showreels*, *Rate Cards*, *Frequently Asked Questions*).
- **`<h3>`**: Sub-items and individual creator names or package titles.
- **Semantic landmarks**: `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`.

---

## High-CTR Title & Description Formulas

```text
Title Formula:
Top [Specialty / Profession] in [City] | [Brand Name] 🇵🇰

Description Formula:
[Action Verb] top verified [Specialty] in [City]. Watch showreels in-app with transparent [Currency] rates and direct [WhatsApp / Contact] with 0% fees.
```
